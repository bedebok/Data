#+TITLE: Medeltidshandskrift 35. Karen Ludvigsdatter's Book of Hours

A book of hours belonging to <<Karen Ludvigsdatter Rosenkrantz>> (d. 1535) around the turn of the sixteenth century. The manuscript follows the /Hortulus Animae/ type and opens as a book of hours with the calendar, Hours of the Virgin, and Seven Penintential Psalms with the Litany. Thereafter on a new codicological unit are various prayers.
